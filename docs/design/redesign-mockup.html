What changes, regardless of direction

Nine layout decisions

1Every touch target clears 44 px. Today Button is px-3 py-1 — about 24 px tall, and every control in the app inherits it. Steppers compact back to 34 px on tablet and desktop, where the input is a mouse.
2Destructive moves out of thumb range. Delete leaves the player row for an overflow menu; Queue becomes the one big tap.
3Start game gets a sticky bar. The most-tapped control of the night stops living mid-page inside the queue card.
4Courts become a snap rail on phone. Six courts don't fit in a vertical stack; they do fit in a swipe.
5"Up next" is stated, not inferred. Today you read it out of blue/red row tinting. Now it names the court it's heading to.
6Skill gets an ordinal badge. Six bands drive who plays whom, so they get a ramp dot and a short label instead of grey lowercase text in brackets.
7The room code leads. It's the whole sharing loop and it's currently 12 px grey.
8Team 1 / Team 2 become Side 1 / Side 2. They aren't teams — they're whoever the auto-pick put on each half of one court.
9Emoji icons become real SVGs. 🏸🔒📝🗑️⚠️ render differently on every platform.
Tokens in globals.css Rewrite ui.tsx primitives Fix the Geist/Arial bug Add dark mode No schema change No RLS change

Deliberately out of scope. Both are Phase 4 conversations.
